免疫学¶

免疫学作为比较单独的一份内容，放在生理学里。由于竞赛期间专门学习免疫较少，因此这部分免疫学以北大3学分免疫学的推荐书目 Cellular and Molecular Immunology 作为蓝本框架，本部分内容大致是该书的精简翻译与整理，并综合课堂讲解内容及其他教材与资料内容。

参考资料¶

  1. Cellular and Molecular Immunology by Abul K. Abbas, Andrew H. H. Lichtman, Shiv Pillai; 10th edition (2022)
  2. Janeway’s Immunobiology by Kenneth Murphy & Casey Weaver, Garland Science; 9 edition (2016)
  3. Kuby Immunology by Owen & Punt & Stranford, W. H. Freeman; 8th edition (2018)
  4. How the Immune System Works by Lauren Sompayrac; 5 edition (2016)
